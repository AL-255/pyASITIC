# GF180MCU stack presets

The process selector includes all **45 cross sections** in GF180 interconnect
specification [section 3](https://gf180mcu-pdk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/analog/layout/inter_specs/inter_specs_3.html):
2–6 routing metals, 6/9/11/30 kÅ top metal, no MIM, MIM A (above M2), and
MIM B (below the top routing metal). Both published MIM gaps are available:
42 nm for nominal 1.5 fF/µm² and 62 nm for 1.0 fF/µm². This produces 73 `.tek`
files for the 45 configurations. The 2-metal configuration supports 30 kÅ
without MIM only, as listed in section 3.45.

These are nominal **coil stack models**, not calibrated RF or MIM PDK models.
They use the original ASITIC solvers. Selecting a stack loads its `.tek` text,
which is retained in project downloads and can be downloaded separately.
An imported `.tek` with a recognized preset filename also restores its metadata.

## Published data

Sources are pinned to the official
[`google/gf180mcu-pdk` revision de3240d](https://github.com/google/gf180mcu-pdk/tree/de3240d7529a6970437ac3344820aaae7839f215).
The generated catalogue records SHA-256 hashes for the 45 cross-section images,
their source pages and the tables used. All drawings were visually checked.

- Metal bottoms follow the section 3 drawings: M1 at 0.86 µm above the silicon
  reference plane, ordinary IMD pitches 1.15 µm, and 1.45 µm at the MIM level.
  Ordinary metal is 0.55 µm thick; the MIM plate is 0.295 µm thick. The MIM plate
  sits above the lower metal's top by the selected SiN thickness.
- Top metal uses drawing values: 0.7025, 1.0025, 1.1925 and 3 µm for 6, 9,
  11 and 30 kÅ. Oxide passivation thicknesses (including the embedded top
  metal) are 1.4025, 2.0025, 2.3925 and 3.3 µm, followed by 0.3 µm nitride.
  **The 11 kÅ drawings differ from the general tables**, which list top metal
  1.25 µm and oxide passivation 2.3 µm. The presets follow the requested drawings
  and expose that choice in the webpage.
- [Section 4 sheet resistance](https://gf180mcu-pdk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/analog/layout/inter_specs/inter_specs_4.html)
  supplies 0.09 Ω/□ for ordinary routing metal, 0.18 Ω/□ for MIM top plate,
  and 0.06/0.04/0.04/0.0083 Ω/□ for the four top-metal options. ASITIC `rsh`
  fields use milliohms per square.
- [Section 2.2](https://gf180mcu-pdk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/analog/layout/inter_specs/inter_specs_2_2.html)
  supplies oxide εr=4 and nitride εr=7. Adjacent oxide regions are represented
  as one region, preserving the drawn heights and oxide passivation boundary.
- [Electrical specification 5.2](https://gf180mcu-pdk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/analog/spice/elec_specs/elec_specs_5_2.html)
  supplies nominal routing-via resistance 4.5 Ω per 0.26 × 0.26 µm cut.
  Via spacing is 0.36 µm to accommodate the ≥4×4-array rule, with 0.12 µm
  enclosure from the resistance-variation guideline in
  [Vian rules](https://gf180mcu-pdk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/physical_verification/design_manual/drm_07_15.html).
  MIM top-plate vias use 0.5 µm spacing and 0.4 µm enclosure; their 4.5 Ω
  resistance is an assumption based on the routing-via nominal value.
- GDS uses published drawn layer/datatype pairs: M1–M6 on
  34/0, 36/0, 42/0, 46/0, 81/0, 53/0; routing vias on
  35/0, 38/0, 40/0, 41/0, 82/0; MIM top plate on 75/0.
  Explicit Python/JSON layer maps override these defaults. Additional MIM
  markers, device rules, metal slotting and other fabrication requirements are
  outside this stream writer.

## Explicit modeling assumptions

The referenced interconnect data do not specify a complete substrate RF model.
Each preset starts with **silicon 10 Ω·cm, 700 µm thick, εr=11.9**. Resistivity
and thickness are editable in the process panel and saved in the `.tek` text.
These values are assumptions, not extracted GF180 substrate specifications.
Dielectric resistivity is assumed 1e10 Ω·cm; the air region is modeled as
100 µm with εr=1. The 512 µm chip and 128² FFT grid are workbench settings.

The planar stack omits local transistor/poly/STI topography, the conformal
liner and the local MIM SiN patch. It includes the **MIM plate's geometry and
sheet resistance**, and the raised IMD level, but treats its surrounding volume
as oxide. Consequently CAP/Pi results involving that plate are not an accurate
prediction of a fabricated MIM capacitor's density. MIM is not treated as an
extra routing layer when choosing the default top/crossover metals.

The process selector covers the configurations in the linked interconnect
specification, rather than conflating them with the open_pdks A/B/C/D labels.
All data and modifications retain the upstream Apache-2.0 attribution in
`src/reasitic/technologies/GF180-NOTICE.txt`.

To regenerate from an official source checkout:

```sh
git -C /path/to/gf180mcu-pdk checkout de3240d7529a6970437ac3344820aaae7839f215
python3 tools/build_gf180.py --docs /path/to/gf180mcu-pdk
```
